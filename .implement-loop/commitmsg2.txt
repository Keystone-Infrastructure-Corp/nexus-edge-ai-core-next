[nexus-pipeline] publish live-view frames at decode rate, not inference rate

The supervisor's analysis loop awaits detect inline, and the only writer of
LatestFrameCache sat after it. On a ten-camera Apollo Lake box that put the
cloud wall at 0.125 fps -- 16x below the 2 fps floor gate.rs documents -- and
flapping stale/live on an 8s beat against the pump's 5s STALL_AFTER.

Worse than the rate: mpsc + try_send drops the NEWEST frame, so a permanently
full 8-slot queue hands the loop a frame eight consumer-cycles old. At 8s per
inference that is ~64s of latency, which is why the wall looked "behind"
rather than merely slow, and it mis-stamps everything keyed on
frame.captured_at.

A tap task now sits between the source and the loop. It publishes every
decoded frame to the cache and forwards latest-wins over a watch channel, so
the loop also analyses the freshest frame instead of a minute-old one.

LatestFrameCache gains a per-camera epoch. abort() is asynchronous, so without
it a tap still draining can repopulate a cache stop_camera just cleared --
streaming a live feed of a disabled camera, strictly worse than the frozen
frame the existing clear() test locks. Same shape as is_current_session
(BUG-070).

AbortOnDrop guards the tap handle: every FrameSource learns the camera is gone
from tx.closed(), which only resolves when the receiver drops, and a bare
JoinHandle detaches rather than aborts. Without the guard the source would
decode at 15 fps forever, Ctrl-C would hang on the GStreamer bus thread, and a
camera edit would open a second RTSP session against firmware documented as
capping at one.

observe_frame moves into the tap -- the true "received from the source" point
its own doc comment describes; inside the loop it measured inference rate.

get_latest_frame_meta reports objects only when objects_frame_id matches the
frame on screen, so it cannot attach boxes to a frame_id/trace_id the detector
never ran on, nor serve boxes in a stale coordinate space after a dims change.

Outcome is ~1 fps, not 4: the pump's scene_changed keys off objects_signature,
which stays on the inference clock, so emission falls through to the 1 Hz
keepalive. The frame's age is the real win -- current instead of ~64s old.

Also fixes wall cells going dark during a detector wedge: supervisor.rs:690
returns Continue on detector error before the post-inference cache write, so
every cell blanked for the whole of BUG-135's 36-minute outage.

BUG-136.
