[nexus-inference] address review: bound the wedged CPU build, fix the fallback EP guard, require a time window

Review of the first pass found that rewriting the fallback chain to ["cpu"]
moved it onto build_or_demote_to_cpu's unbounded path, because the CPU-only
short-circuit sits above the ACCELERATOR_WEDGED check. After a BUG-120 wedge the
abandoned thread may hold an ORT process-global lock, so that build could hang
startup outright -- strictly worse than the outage being fixed. The latch is now
checked first, with a test that a CPU-only chain stays bounded once wedged.

The fallback guard keyed on "contains cpu" was wrong twice over: every shipped
Hailo config is ["hailo", "cpu"], so it rewrote the one chain it was meant to
protect, while a hand-pinned ["gpu"] was skipped and left unfixed even though
ORT appends cpu(fallback) to it anyway. It now keys on the chain not naming
hailo, matched case-insensitively after trim() the way selected_for_priority
normalises.

Demotion now requires 32 consecutive failures AND 15s elapsed. 32 frames is
under a second on one worker serving ten cameras, and demotion is permanent
until restart -- the i915 reset at 18:12 that night recovered on its own, and a
count-only rule would have permanently demoted a healthy accelerator over it.
The streak is cleared on demotion and the state change is edge-triggered, so a
slot a future supervisor restores can demote again instead of latching past the
threshold forever.

Also reverts the unrelated .gitignore change from the first commit.
